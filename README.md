# Tata Power EV Charging Network Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

As electric vehicle adoption continues to grow, charging infrastructure needs to keep up with changing customer demand.

In this project, I built an **Excel-based dashboard for Tata Power's EV charging network** to understand charging demand, station utilization, customer behavior, and financial performance.

The main goal was to turn raw EV charging data into clear business insights that can help in **operational improvement, customer analysis, and future charging infrastructure expansion**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The dashboard focuses on four major areas:

* Charging demand and usage trends
* Charging station utilization and operational efficiency
* Customer behavior and demand patterns
* Financial performance and expansion opportunities

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Data Cleaning
* Data Analysis
* Excel Formulas
* Pivot Tables
* Data Visualization
* Dashboard Design
* Business & Strategic Analysis

## 📌 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Business & Usage Overview

This section provides an overall view of charging activity across the network.

The analysis looks at:

* Charging sessions
* Energy consumption
* Revenue generation
* Active customers
* Average revenue per session
* Average energy consumed per session
* Peak charging hours

The analysis shows a steady increase in charging demand, indicating growing EV adoption and higher network utilization.

## ⚡ 2. Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency

This section focuses on how efficiently charging stations and plugs are being used across different locations.

The analysis includes:

* Station-wise charging activity
* Energy consumption by district
* Plug utilization
* Sessions per plug
* Identification of highly utilized stations
* Identification of underutilized stations

Locations such as **Indiranagar and Rajajinagar** show strong charging activity and energy consumption, while plug utilization helps identify locations where additional infrastructure may be required.

## 👥 3. Customer & Demand Behavior

The customer analysis focuses on understanding who is using the charging network and how their charging behavior differs.

Key areas include:

* Customer income segments
* EV models
* Battery capacity
* Charging sessions per customer
* Revenue per customer
* Energy consumption

The analysis indicates that **mid-range income customers contribute significantly to charging revenue and energy consumption**. Battery capacity and EV models also show differences in charging demand and usage behavior.

## 💰 4. Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy

The final section connects operational performance with financial decision-making.

The analysis includes:

* ROI
* Payback period
* Annual revenue
* EBITDA
* Demand-supply gaps
* District-level expansion opportunities

Districts such as **Banashankari and Jayanagar** show stronger long-term investment returns. The analysis also highlights locations where future EV growth may exceed existing charging capacity.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

Some of the major insights from the dashboard include:

* Charging demand is showing a steady growth trend.
* Peak charging hours can help improve infrastructure planning.
* Some charging stations are operating at high utilization and may require additional capacity.
* Station-wise analysis helps identify both high-performing and underutilized locations.
* Mid-range income customers contribute significantly to charging demand and revenue.
* Higher battery capacity vehicles generally result in greater charging energy consumption.
* High-ROI districts can be prioritized for future charging infrastructure.
* Demand-supply gap analysis can help identify locations requiring capacity expansion.
